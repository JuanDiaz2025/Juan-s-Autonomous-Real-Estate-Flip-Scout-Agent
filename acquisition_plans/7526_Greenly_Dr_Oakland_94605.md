Takeaway: **strong on a cosmetic/light rehab, thin on a gut job.** The deal's
whole risk is *which rehab scenario is real* — so the rehab scope is the single
most important thing to verify (see §4/§8).

## 7. Offer Strategy & Terms (Training §10 — terms can beat price)

Recommended posture: **credible all-cash, as-is, fast & flexible close.**
- **Price:** open at/near list ($600k) contingent on the comp + rehab
  verification above; hold a walk-away at ~$636k MAO (targets ~$150k net on a
  realistic mid-scope $100/sqft rehab). Do not chase past ~$640k.
- **Terms to lead with:** cash, proof of funds attached, as-is (no repair
  requests), short/no inspection contingency *only after* our own quick
  inspection, no appraisal contingency, flexible close date + rent-back if the
  seller needs it. These matter more than a few thousand in price to an estate
  seller who wants certainty.
- Never promise a term that hasn't been approved internally.
