## 5. DATA HYGIENE & CONFIDENCE
- **Strongest records**: have both a settlement statement and an expense sheet (e.g., 255 Amber, 819 S Mary, 1416 Solana, 2448 Moraine, 24678 Joyce)
- **Weaker**: web‑only sale figures, or sale‑known / purchase‑unknown
- **Excluded as non‑flips**: cancelled deals (3475 N Main, 3984 Roesner, 7601 Weld, 36500 Alder), offers/leads, rentals held
- **Important**: Gross ≠ Net – financing, holding, commissions, and staging still need to be layered. Thin "wins" may be breakeven‑to‑loss on a true net basis.
